# Content Guidelines

## Identity

Signalist is a COMMENTARY channel, not a clip channel. Every video must contain original analysis that transforms the source material. The text cards ARE the product — the interview footage is evidence supporting our commentary.

## Transformative Use Standard

Each Shorts must pass this test: if you remove all text cards and just play the interview clips, the video should feel incomplete and lack its core message. Our commentary must add meaning that didn't exist in the original.

Examples of transformative commentary:

- Fact-checking a speaker's claim with data they didn't mention
- Adding timeline context ("He said this 3 months before X happened")
- Presenting a counterpoint or alternative interpretation
- Connecting the statement to a broader trend the speaker doesn't address
- Reframing a technical statement for a general audience

NOT transformative:

- Atmospheric text ("Everything is about to change")
- Restating what the speaker just said in different words
- Generic commentary ("This is important")

## Prohibited

- Political propaganda or one-sided partisan content without acknowledging complexity
- Content that could incite violence or hatred
- Misleading out-of-context clips that distort the speaker's intent
- Pure clip reposts with no commentary value added
- Using footage from channels that actively DMCA commentary/reaction content

## Required Elements

- Every Shorts must include source attribution in description (channel name + video title)
- At least 3 commentary text cards per video (opening + transitions + closing)
- Commentary must add factual context, analysis, or original perspective
- Closing card must present our take — not just a vague question

## Copyright Safety

- Preferred sources: podcasts, conference talks, congressional hearings, CC-licensed content, channels that welcome commentary
- Each clip segment should be the minimum needed to support the commentary point
- Total source footage should not exceed 70% of the video — commentary cards fill the rest
- Always link to original video in description

## Format Requirements

- Duration: up to 3 minutes (YouTube Shorts max)
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical
- Minimum 3 commentary text cards
- Source footage never plays uninterrupted for more than 30 seconds
